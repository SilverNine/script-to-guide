## [intro]
This guide shows you how to make your first diagram in MermanDraw. It takes about two minutes, and all you need is a browser.
**(screen ▶ title card)**

## [plan]
You will do four things. Write the code, pick a theme, export the picture, and send a link.
**(screen ▶ step list, one item lit per verb)**

## [write]
Open the editor at mermandraw.com. The code goes on the left. Each line connects two boxes with an arrow, and the text in brackets is the label. The preview on the right redraws the diagram while you type. Here, a decision sends the answer back to the start.
**(screen ▶ write.webp: spotlight code pane, then line 2 with a callout on the label, then the preview, then the decision)**

## [theme]
Next, open the Theme menu. There are seven themes, from Clean to Mono. Choose Neon, and the preview changes at once. Your code stays exactly the same.
**(screen ▶ cursor clicks Theme, swap to theme-menu.webp, spotlight the menu, click Neon, swap to neon.webp, spotlight the code)**

## [export]
When it looks right, export it. SVG stays sharp at any size, so it suits documents and slides. PNG works anywhere an image does.
**(screen ▶ magnifier on SVG and PNG, cursor clicks PNG)**

## [link]
To send it, press Link. The address it copies carries the whole diagram, so nothing is stored and there is no account to make. Share is the other choice. It makes a public page with a preview image for chat apps.
**(screen ▶ cursor clicks Link, swap to link.webp with the "copied" message magnified, then magnifier on Share)**

## [end]
That is the whole loop. Write, theme, export, and send. Try it now at mermandraw.com.
**(screen ▶ closing card)**
